# MY THAI Remedial Massage — Website

A modern, responsive single-page website for **MY THAI Remedial Massage,
North Strathfield**. Everything (HTML, CSS, and JavaScript) lives in a single
self-contained file — `index.html` — for easy testing and deployment.

## Open it

Just open `index.html` in any browser. No build step, no dependencies.

## What's included

- **Sticky navigation bar** with a mobile hamburger menu and a "Call Now" CTA.
- **Hero section** with the welcome copy, a prominent *Call Now* button, and a
  services highlight card.
- **About & Services** — personalised treatments covering stress & tension
  relief, back pain & headache management, improved sleep & energy, and enhanced
  flexibility & mobility.
- **Health Funds** section highlighting **HICAPS Available** and **Instant
  Health Fund Claims for Remedial Massage**.
- **Locations** — Sans Souci, Kogarah, Glebe, Sylvania, Hurstville, and North
  Strathfield.
- **Footer** with operating hours (Mon–Sun, 10:00 AM – 8:00 PM), phone
  `(02) 8355 1700`, and accepted payments (Credit, Debit, American Express).

## Design

A premium, relaxing palette of **black, gold, and soft warm accents** to match
the storefront's black sign with gold/white lettering and pink/orange floral
logo. Fully mobile-responsive with scroll-reveal animations.
