# The Executive Travel & Jet-Lag Protocol

For the operator who performs across time zones. The goal: arrive functional, protect the first night's sleep, and hold your fundamentals when everything else is chaos.

> ⚠️ Educational only, not medical advice. Consult your physician, especially regarding any sleep aids or medication.

## Before you fly
- **Shift toward the destination** 1–2 days early where possible (wake/sleep 30–60 min closer to destination time).
- **Front-load sleep** — arrive with a full tank, not a deficit.
- **Pack the essentials of your OS:** eye mask, earplugs, a resistance band, electrolytes, your wearable.

## On the plane
- **Set your watch to the destination** on boarding and mentally live in that time zone.
- **Hydrate; go easy on alcohol** (it wrecks sleep quality and worsens jet lag).
- **Move** — walk and stretch periodically.
- **Time your sleep to the destination night**, not your origin.

## On arrival
- **Get outdoor daylight** as soon as possible — the single most powerful tool to reset your clock.
- **Eat on the destination schedule** to entrain your body.
- **Protect the first night:** cool, dark room; wind-down runway; consistent lights-out.
- **Move gently** the first day (a walk, light training) to boost alertness.

## The "Minimum Viable Day" on the road
When the schedule detonates, keep only these:
1. **Fixed wake time + morning light**
2. **A 10-minute walk** after your biggest meal
3. **Protein-first** at least once
4. **One breathing reset** before your most important meeting

That's the floor you never drop below. Everything else is a bonus.
