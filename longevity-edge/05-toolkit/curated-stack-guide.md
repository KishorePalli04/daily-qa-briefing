# The Curated Stack — Signal vs. Noise

The high performer's problem isn't access — it's *filtering*. This guide is deliberately minimalist: buy what earns its place against your time and attention; ignore the rest.

> ⚠️ Educational only, not medical or product advice. No affiliations implied. Discuss supplements, tests, and devices with a qualified physician. Categories are described generically on purpose.

**The filter (apply to everything):**
1. Does it improve a **fundamental** (sleep, movement, nutrition, stress, or measurement)?
2. Is it **evidence-supported**, not just marketed?
3. Will you **actually use it** consistently?

If it fails any of the three, it's noise.

---

## 🟢 Generally worth it (improves a fundamental)

| Category | Why it earns its place | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| A sleep-optimised bedroom | Sleep is the #1 lever | Blackout, cooler temperature, quiet. Environment > gadgets |
| A wearable you'll actually wear | Turns feelings into trends | Pick one; consistency matters more than which |
| A way to strength-train reliably | Muscle = healthspan | Home rack, gym membership, or a good trainer — whatever you'll use |
| Real-food nutrition | Foundation of energy & metabolism | Spend here before any supplement |
| A short CGM trial | Makes your glucose response undeniable | With physician guidance; behaviour change is the ROI |
| Comprehensive bloodwork (periodic) | Baseline + trend | Order/interpret with your doctor |

## 🟡 Situational (only if a test or physician justifies it)

| Category | When it may make sense |
|---|---|
| Targeted supplements | Only to correct a *measured* gap (e.g., low vitamin D) — not blanket "stacks" |
| Recovery tools (sauna/cold, massage) | If you'll use them and enjoy them; pleasant, not magic |
| Advanced imaging / diagnostics | Discuss risk/benefit with a physician; can be valuable, can be over-sold |

## 🔴 Usually noise (be skeptical)

- "Miracle" compounds and exotic gadgets sold mainly via podcast hype
- Anything promising a **shortcut around the fundamentals**
- 20-supplement "stacks" with no testing behind them
- Devices you'll use twice and abandon

---

## The 80/20 truth
The overwhelming majority of your result comes from **sleep, movement, nutrition, stress, and measurement** — most of which cost little or nothing. The stack should *support* the fundamentals, never replace them. When in doubt, spend on behaviour, not on gadgets.

> **Rule of thumb:** For every new thing you add, remove one that isn't earning its place.
