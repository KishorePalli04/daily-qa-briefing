# Short-Form Video Scripts (Reels / Shorts / TikTok)

Cinematic, understated, authority-led. Format: [VISUAL] + spoken/on-screen text. Keep 20–45s. End with a soft CTA + disclaimer caption.

> ⚠️ Put "Educational only, not medical advice" in every caption. Avoid medical claims on screen.

---

## Production notes
- **Aesthetic:** dark, cinematic, shallow depth of field, warm-cool contrast, marble/wood/linen textures. Match the obsidian-green + champagne brand.
- **Music:** restrained, cinematic, low tempo. No trending meme audio — it cheapens the premium positioning.
- **Text:** elegant serif on screen, minimal words, lots of negative space.
- **Pace:** slower than typical reels. Confidence reads as calm.
