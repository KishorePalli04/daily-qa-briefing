# Paid Ad Scripts

Copy for paid campaigns. Keep the premium, understated tone. Always include the disclaimer. Wire each to the sales page.

> ⚠️ Platforms restrict health/medical claims and sometimes "personal attributes" targeting. Keep language about lifestyle, energy, focus and habits — never disease, treatment, or "before/after body" claims. Get legal review before spending at scale.

## Targeting notes (organic-first recommended)
- This audience is expensive and skeptical on paid — **lead with organic authority content** (essays, threads, reels) to warm them, then retarget engagers.
- Best paid audiences: job titles (Founder, CEO, Partner, MD, Investor), interests (executive health, longevity, biohacking, private aviation, wealth management), and lookalikes from your email list / engagers.
- **Retarget** sales-page visitors and video-viewers with the scarcity/close ad (Ad B).
