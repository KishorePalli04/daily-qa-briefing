# Social Media Strategy — Selling to High-Income Buyers

The wealthy don't buy from hype. They buy from **authority, discretion, proof, and identity**. Everything here is engineered for that psychology.

## The 5 rules of marketing to high performers
1. **Sell the identity, not the features.** They're buying "a serious operator who has their health handled," not "a course."
2. **Understate everything.** Quiet confidence out-converts loud claims. No 🔥🔥🔥, no fake urgency, no shouting.
3. **Lead with the cost of inaction.** Their pain isn't price — it's decline, lost years, a health scare. Frame against that, not against other courses.
4. **Anchor to the $25k clinic.** Position as the accessible-but-premium alternative, never as "cheap."
5. **Exclusivity > availability.** "Founding cohort," "application," "capped" — scarcity signals quality to this buyer.

## Platform plan
| Platform | Why | Content focus |
|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn** | Where founders/execs/investors live | Authority essays, founder health stories, data, "operator" framing |
| **Instagram** | Aspirational lifestyle + reach | Cinematic reels, carousels, the aesthetic, dashboards |
| **X (Twitter)** | Founder/tech/biohacker discourse | Threads, contrarian takes, evidence, curation |
| **YouTube (long + Shorts)** | Deep trust, high-ticket closes | Masterclass previews, "what the $25k clinic won't tell you" |

## The 3 content pillars (rotate)
1. **Educate** — one high-signal, evidence-led idea (builds authority & trust).
2. **Prove** — data, biomarkers, transformations, "here's what actually moved the numbers."
3. **Identity** — stories & beliefs that make the buyer think *"this is for people like me."*

## The funnel
**Attention (reels/threads)** → **Trust (carousels/essays/long video)** → **Desire (the $25k-clinic anchor + founding-cohort scarcity)** → **Action (application-style CTA → sales page → checkout).**

## ⚠️ Compliance (critical for a health product)
- **No medical claims.** Never say it treats, cures, prevents, or diagnoses anything. Speak in terms of energy, focus, sleep, fitness, habits, and "how you feel."
- **Always add a disclaimer** on health content: *"Educational only, not medical advice. Consult your physician."*
- **No fake scarcity or fake testimonials.** Mark illustrative examples clearly until you have real, permissioned ones.
- **Substantiate stats.** Only cite the researched figures (e.g., HNW longevity priority, clinic pricing) with sources.
