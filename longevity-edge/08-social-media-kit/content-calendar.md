# 30-Day Launch Content Calendar

> ⚠️ Add "Educational only, not medical advice" to health posts.

## Cadence tips
- **Give real value 4 posts for every 1 pitch.** This buyer punishes pitch-heavy feeds.
- **Repurpose relentlessly:** one essay → a carousel → a reel script → a thread → an email.
- **Only use scarcity that's true.** A real cap protects the brand; a fake one destroys it.
- **Email is your closer.** Social warms; the list converts. Build it from day 1 (waitlist).
