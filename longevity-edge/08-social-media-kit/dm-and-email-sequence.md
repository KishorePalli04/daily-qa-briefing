# DM & Email Sequences

For warming leads and closing the sale. Keep the premium, low-pressure, peer-to-peer tone. Never spammy — this buyer blocks pushy.

> ⚠️ Educational only, not medical advice. No cold-DM spam; use these for people who engaged/opted in.

---

## Part 1 — Warm DM sequence (for engaged prospects / waitlist)

**DM 1 — value first (no pitch)**
> Hey [Name] — saw you engaged with the post on executive recovery. Genuinely curious: what's your biggest constraint right now — sleep, energy, or just time to figure out what actually works? (No pitch, I just think about this a lot.)

**DM 2 — the soft bridge (after they reply)**
> That's the exact thing I built The Longevity Edge to solve — one vetted system instead of the podcast noise, 30 min a day. Founding cohort's opening soon and it's capped. Want me to send you the details before it's public?

**DM 3 — the invite**
> Here you go: [link]. Founding price is $997 (rises to $1,500 after). 60-day guarantee, so there's no real downside to trying it. Happy to answer anything. *Educational only — not medical advice.*
