# Market Research — 10 High-Income Digital Product Ideas

**Target market:** High-income earners & high-net-worth individuals (founders, executives, investors, surgeons, lawyers, top consultants), ~35–55, cash-rich but time-poor.

## Why this market (2026 research)
- **Longevity is "the new luxury."** Over **90%** of wealthy individuals now cite longevity as a critical planning priority, and wellness/longevity is a defining driver of ultra-high-net-worth spending. *(Bank of America Private Bank; Sotheby's/PR Newswire, 2026)*
- **Executive-health & biohacking is booming.** Premium executive-health programs run **$2,000–$25,000/year**; longevity is one of the most commercially important premium lifestyle categories of the decade. *(World Longevity Clinics; Elevated Magazine, 2026)*
- **The wealthy buy outcomes, not content.** High earners evaluate offers as *investments/leverage* — when perceived ROI is high, price resistance drops. *(Deadline Funnel; Sellfy, 2026)*
- **They value time and status above price.** Experiences, exceptionalism, and "the edge" outrank possessions.

## The 10 ideas

| # | Product idea | Format | Core promise | High-income appeal |
|---|--------------|--------|--------------|--------------------|
| 1 | **The Longevity Edge** | Masterclass + protocols + trackers | Add healthy, high-energy years and out-perform at the top — without living in a clinic | ⭐ Longevity = the #1 HNW priority; status + ROI on their most valuable asset (time/health) |
| 2 | The Private Investor's Playbook (private markets & alternatives) | Course + models | Access & evaluate private-market deals like a family office | Directly money-linked; but heavier compliance/regulatory risk |
| 3 | The Personal Brand Authority System | Course + templates | Turn your expertise into influence & inbound high-ticket opportunities | Every high earner wants authority; sells natively on social |
| 4 | The Executive Time & Energy OS | System + templates | Reclaim 10+ hrs/week and run your life like an operator | Time is their scarcest asset |
| 5 | The Family Office Blueprint (wealth structuring, education-only) | Guide + checklists | Organise, protect & pass on wealth | High value; compliance-sensitive |
| 6 | The Peak Cognitive Performance Protocol | Mini-course + tracker | Sharper focus, deeper work, less burnout | Performance edge; subset of #1 |
| 7 | The Elite Sleep System | Guide + tracker | Fix executive sleep, wake up recovered | Huge pain; subset of #1 |
| 8 | The Curated Longevity Stack (tools/tests/devices guide) | Premium buyer's guide | Skip the noise — the vetted stack that's worth it | Concierge/curation they'll pay to save time |
| 9 | The High-Performer's Nutrition Framework | Guide + meal templates | Eat for energy & healthspan without dieting | Evergreen; subset of #1 |
| 10 | The Legacy & Meaning Playbook | Guide + prompts | Design a life of purpose beyond the next milestone | Purpose-driven spending trend |

## Chosen product

**#1 — The Longevity Edge** — *The Peak-Performance Operating System for High Performers.*
It sits squarely on the strongest 2026 HNW trend (longevity), carries obvious status and ROI ("your health is your ultimate asset"), performs brilliantly on social, and is a natural **combination product** (masterclass + protocols + trackers) that absorbs ideas #4, #6, #7, #8, #9 as modules/bonuses. It's positioned as the **$1k system that delivers what busy high-earners want from a $25k clinic** — minus the waiting rooms.

> **Positioning note:** This is an *education & lifestyle* product, not medical care. All materials are framed as evidence-informed education with clear disclaimers to consult a qualified physician.
