# The Customer Avatar

## Meet "Apex Marcus"

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Marcus Hale |
| **Age** | 46 |
| **Role** | Founder & CEO of a $40M SaaS company (bootstrapped, took minority PE) |
| **Net worth** | ~$8M (mostly equity + property) |
| **Location** | Lives between Sydney and San Francisco |
| **Family** | Wife (a GP), two kids (11 and 14) |
| **Health tech** | Whoop + Oura, a Peloton he ignores, a drawer full of supplements |
| **Income** | $600k salary + distributions |
| **Dream** | To be as sharp and energetic at 60 as he was at 35 — and to walk his daughter down the aisle strong |
| **Fear** | That the pace that built his success is quietly killing him — like it did his father at 58 |

**One-line summary:** Marcus has won at business but is losing at biology. He's tired, sleeping badly, watching his bloodwork drift, and drowning in conflicting longevity advice with zero time to filter it. He doesn't want a bootcamp — he wants a precise, evidence-led *operating system* he can run in 30 minutes a day, built for someone with his constraints and standards.

## How the product answers Marcus
- **No time →** a 30-minute-a-day operating system, not a lifestyle overhaul
- **Overwhelm →** one vetted, evidence-led system replaces 100 podcasts
- **Wasted spend →** the curated stack tells him what's worth it and what's noise
- **Fear of decline →** measurable biomarkers + recovery he can *see* improve
- **Status/standards →** premium, discreet, "for serious operators" — matches his identity
