# Ideal Customer — Demographics, Psychographics & Pain Points

**Product:** The Longevity Edge
**Market:** High-income / high-net-worth high performers

## Demographics
- **Age:** 35–55 (core 40–50)
- **Income / wealth:** $250k+ household income, or $1M–$25M net worth; includes founders post-exit, senior execs, partners (law/consulting/medicine), investors, established creators
- **Gender:** ~60% male / 40% female (female longevity/wellness segment growing fast)
- **Location:** Global, English-speaking wealth hubs — US, UK, Australia, UAE, Singapore, Canada
- **Roles:** Founder/CEO, C-suite, MD/Partner, portfolio investor, surgeon, elite professional
- **Life stage:** Peak career + demanding family life; "successful but running on fumes"
- **Tech:** Wears an Oura/Whoop/Apple Watch; owns the gadgets; overwhelmed by conflicting advice

## Psychographics
- **Values:** Achievement, excellence, autonomy, optimisation, legacy, being early to an edge
- **Identity:** "High performer." Prides themselves on discipline in business — quietly ashamed their health has slipped
- **Aspirations:** Sustained energy, sharp mind, look/feel a decade younger, be there for their kids and grandkids, extend *peak* years (healthspan, not just lifespan)
- **Deepest driver:** Fear of decline — of being outrun by younger operators, of the health scare that ended a peer's career, of "having everything but the health to enjoy it"
- **Buying behaviour:** Will pay a premium for *curation, certainty, and time saved.* Skeptical of hype and "gurus." Wants credibility, discretion, and a done-for-you system. Compares price to the cost of a health crisis, not to other courses
- **Status signals that convert:** exclusivity, "for serious operators," data/evidence, a private room feel, understatement over hype

## Core Pain Points
1. **Chronically low energy** despite success — the 3pm crash, the fog
2. **Poor, fragmented sleep** — wired, travelling, waking at 3am
3. **Creeping decline** — weight, bloodwork drifting, less resilience each year
4. **Information overload** — 100 podcasts, biohackers, contradictory advice; no system
5. **No time** — can't add a 2-hour daily wellness routine to an already full life
6. **Stress & burnout** — always "on," cortisol-fuelled, no real recovery
7. **The health scare in their circle** — a peer's heart attack/cancer as a wake-up call
8. **Guilt & shame** — disciplined everywhere except their own body
9. **Wasted spend** — thousands on supplements, gadgets, trainers with no clear ROI
10. **Fear of missing their peak years** — "what's the point of the wealth if I'm too depleted to enjoy it?"

## What they're NOT
Not looking for a generic diet or a bootcamp. Not motivated by "lose 5kg." They want an **operating system for their body and mind** that fits a high-stakes life — precise, efficient, evidence-led, and premium.
