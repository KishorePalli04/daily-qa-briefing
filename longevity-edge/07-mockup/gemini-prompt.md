# "Gemini-Ready" Mockup Prompt

Copy-paste into Gemini (or any image AI) to generate a 16:9 (1280×720) premium promotional mockup. A fully working, editable version already ships as `07-mockup/mockup.html` — open it in a browser to view/screenshot at exactly 1280×720.

---

## ✅ Copy-paste prompt

```
Create a luxurious, premium promotional mockup image for a high-ticket digital product, dimensions 16:9 (1280x720 pixels).

PRODUCT: "The Longevity Edge" — a peak-performance and longevity operating system for wealthy, high-performing executives, founders and investors (ages 40-55). It includes a video masterclass, protocols, and tracking dashboards.

AUDIENCE: High-net-worth high performers. The design must feel exclusive, discreet, understated and expensive — like a private members' club or a Swiss longevity clinic, NOT a loud fitness ad.

STYLE & MOOD: Editorial luxury. Quiet confidence. Cinematic, dark, sophisticated, lots of negative space. Think Aesop, Loro Piana, a private bank.

COLOURS: A deep obsidian-green background (#0b1310 to #0f1a15 gradient), warm ivory text (#efece0), a refined jade-green primary accent (#4f9c7a), and a restrained champagne-gold accent (#c9a86a) for fine lines and the eyebrow label. High contrast but never garish. Add a soft jade glow top-left and a faint champagne glow bottom-right.

FONTS: An elegant high-contrast SERIF for the headline (Palatino / Garamond / Canela feel), set large with an italic accent word. A clean sans-serif for small supporting text. Uppercase eyebrow labels with wide letter-spacing.

LAYOUT:
- A thin champagne hairline frame inset from the edges (premium border).
- TOP-RIGHT: small champagne text "FOUNDING COHORT · MMXXVI".
- LEFT SIDE: an uppercase champagne eyebrow "FOR HIGH PERFORMERS", a short champagne rule, then a huge serif headline "The Longevity Edge" with the word "Longevity" in italic champagne. Below it a light-grey subheadline "The peak-performance operating system — the $25k clinic, in 30 minutes a day." Three bordered pill tags: "Masterclass", "Protocols", "Dashboards". A solid jade-green call-to-action button "Claim Your Edge" with tiny text under it "60-day guarantee". A muted line "Private founding cohort · Lifetime access".
- RIGHT SIDE: an elegant dark UI card titled "Recovery Dashboard" showing refined metric rows (Sleep score 88 ▲, Resting HR 52 ▼, HRV 71 ▲) in jade, and a smaller tilted card showing "30 min" in large serif with a circular progress ring — suggesting a premium health app.

IMAGERY: Subtle only — faint diagonal contour/topographic lines in the background (like a longevity curve), soft glows. No stock-photo people. Restraint is the point.

Output a single polished, expensive-looking 1280x720 image.
```

---

## 🎨 Variations to try
- **Obsidian + platinum** version (cooler, more "clinical tech") for a male-skewing audience.
- **Warm stone/ivory light** luxury version (Aesop-like) for a female-skewing wellness audience.
- **9:16 story/reel** version (1080×1920) for Instagram/TikTok ads — headline top, dashboard card centre, CTA bottom.
