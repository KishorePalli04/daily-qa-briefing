# The Longevity Edge 🕊️

A complete, **high-ticket digital product** built for **high-income buyers** — designed to be launched and sold on social media.

**Product:** *The Longevity Edge — The Peak-Performance Operating System for High Performers.*
**Market:** High-net-worth / high-income high performers (founders, executives, investors, elite professionals, 35–55).
**Type:** Combination product — 🎬 masterclass + 📕 protocol playbook + 📊 dashboards + 🧪 curated stack + 🕊️ private community.
**Positioning:** "The fundamentals behind the $25k executive clinic, in 30 minutes a day."

## Why this product (grounded in 2026 research)
- **Longevity is "the new luxury"** — >90% of wealthy individuals now rank it a top priority. *(Bank of America Private Bank 2026)*
- **Executive-health clinics charge $2k–$25k/year** — a perfect price anchor for a premium digital product. *(World Longevity Clinics 2026)*
- **High earners buy outcomes, status, certainty and time saved**, not features — and evaluate price against the cost of decline, not other courses.

## 🎨 Design direction
Deliberately **not** the bright startup look of the first product. This is **editorial luxury**: obsidian-green ground, warm ivory text, **jade** as the primary accent with **champagne** as a restrained metallic, and a serif display face. Quiet confidence — because that's what converts high-income buyers.
