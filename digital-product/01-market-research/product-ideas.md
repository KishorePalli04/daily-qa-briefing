# Prompt 1 — 10 Trending Digital Product Ideas

**Target audience:** Australian freelancers, creators & side-hustlers (aged ~25–45) who want to use AI to win clients and build a profitable one-person business without burning out.

Below are 10 high-demand digital product ideas for this audience, each with the format, the core promise, and why it sells right now.

| # | Product idea | Format | Core promise | Why it sells in Australia (2026) |
|---|--------------|--------|--------------|----------------------------------|
| 1 | **The AI Freelance Freedom Accelerator** | Video course + templates + trackers | Land your first (or next) high-paying client using AI in 30 days | Freelancing is surging post-remote-work; everyone wants AI leverage but is overwhelmed |
| 2 | AI Client-Getting Prompt Vault | PDF + Notion prompt library | 200 copy-paste prompts to find, pitch & close clients | Prompts are the #1 "quick win" impulse buy |
| 3 | The Solo Business Financials Tracker | Google Sheets / Excel template | Track income, GST, expenses & tax set-aside in one place | ATO/GST compliance is a real, recurring Aussie pain |
| 4 | Cold Outreach Message Kit | Swipe file (PDF + Docs) | Fill-in-the-blank DMs & emails that book calls | Outreach anxiety is universal for new freelancers |
| 5 | AI Content Engine for Freelancers | Notion system + video walkthrough | Turn 1 idea into 30 days of LinkedIn/IG content | Content = inbound leads; time is the bottleneck |
| 6 | Freelance Rate & Proposal Calculator | Interactive spreadsheet | Price projects with confidence & stop undercharging | Underpricing is the #1 regret of Aussie freelancers |
| 7 | The 6-Figure Portfolio Playbook | eBook + templates | Build a portfolio that converts browsers into buyers | Portfolios directly gate income |
| 8 | AI Productivity OS for Solopreneurs | Notion template | Run your whole business from one dashboard | "Second brain" trend + solo overwhelm |
| 9 | Client Onboarding & Offboarding Kit | Docs + email templates | Look agency-level from day one | Professionalism drives referrals & retention |
| 10 | The Weekend Side-Hustle Launch Plan | 30-page guide + tracker | Launch a paid service in one weekend using AI | "Start now" energy for employed people testing the waters |

## Chosen product (used for the rest of this workflow)

**Idea #1 — The AI Freelance Freedom Accelerator.** It is a *combination* product (video course + templates + trackers), which is exactly what Prompt 4 asks for, and it naturally absorbs several of the other ideas above as bonuses (prompt vault, financials tracker, outreach kit, rate calculator).
