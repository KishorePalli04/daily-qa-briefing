# Prompt 2 — Ideal Customer: Demographics, Psychographics & Pain Points

**Product:** The AI Freelance Freedom Accelerator
**Market:** Australia

## Demographics
- **Age range:** 25–45 (core: 28–38)
- **Gender:** ~55% female / 45% male
- **Location:** Metro & regional AU — Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth, Adelaide, plus a growing regional/remote cohort (Gold Coast, Newcastle, Byron, Geelong)
- **Income:** AUD $45k–$95k (either employed and side-hustling, or newly self-employed and inconsistent)
- **Education:** TAFE diploma to bachelor's degree; self-taught skills are common
- **Job titles / situations:**
  - Freelance graphic/web designer, copywriter, VA, social media manager
  - Marketing coordinator wanting to go solo
  - New parent seeking flexible income
  - Recently redundant corporate worker
  - Tradie's partner running the "back office" who wants their own thing
  - Uni student / recent grad building a side income
- **Tech comfort:** Medium. Uses ChatGPT casually but not systematically. Owns a laptop + phone.

## Psychographics
- **Values:** Freedom, flexibility, autonomy, "being their own boss," lifestyle over status
- **Aspirations:** Replace or top up a salary; work from anywhere; escape the 9–5 and commute; more time with family
- **Identity:** Sees themselves as creative/capable but "not techy" and "not a salesperson"
- **Interests:** Personal development, productivity, side hustles, travel, coffee culture, small-business podcasts, "day in the life" creator content
- **Media:** Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube; follows AU creators & business coaches; listens to podcasts on the commute
- **Buying behaviour:** Buys courses & templates impulsively when the promise is specific and the price is under ~$200; skeptical of hype; wants proof and "done-for-you" shortcuts
- **Objections:** "Is AI going to make my skill worthless?", "Will this actually work in Australia?", "I don't have time", "I've bought courses before and never finished them"

## Core Pain Points
1. **Inconsistent income** — feast-or-famine months, no predictable pipeline
2. **Can't find clients** — doesn't know where to look or how to stand out
3. **Hates selling** — freezes at pitching, discounting out of fear
4. **Undercharging** — resentful and exhausted, working for too little
5. **Overwhelm with AI** — knows AI is important but drowning in tools and hype
6. **Time poverty** — juggling job/kids/hustle, no hours to "figure it out"
7. **Imposter syndrome** — "who am I to charge that?"
8. **No system** — everything lives in their head; chaos and dropped balls
9. **Tax/GST anxiety** — unsure about ABN, GST threshold, set-aside for tax
10. **Fear of AI replacing them** — needs to become the person who *uses* AI, not the one replaced by it
