# Prompt 3 — The Customer Avatar

## Meet "Freelance Freedom Chloe"

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Chloe Bennett |
| **Age** | 32 |
| **Location** | Newcastle, NSW (moved out of Sydney for lifestyle) |
| **Job title** | Freelance graphic designer & social media manager (18 months in) |
| **Prior role** | Marketing coordinator at a mid-size agency (made redundant) |
| **Income** | ~$58k/yr, but wildly inconsistent month to month |
| **Household** | Partner (tradie), one toddler, a rescue kelpie, a mortgage |
| **Devices** | MacBook Air, iPhone, an iPad she barely uses |
| **Tools** | Canva, Instagram, a messy Google Drive, ChatGPT (free, occasional) |
| **Dream** | A calm, predictable $8–10k/month business she runs in school hours from home |
| **Fear** | That AI makes her design/content skills worthless before she "makes it" |

**One-line summary:** Chloe is a talented, lifestyle-driven Aussie freelancer who is *good at the work* but stuck at the *business* — she can't find enough clients, undercharges the ones she has, and secretly worries AI will pass her by. She wants a proven, Australia-relevant system that turns AI into her unfair advantage.

---

## The 20 Things Chloe Is Seeing, Saying, Hearing & Thinking

### 👀 Seeing
1. Other freelancers on Instagram posting "$15k month" screenshots
2. Her bank balance dipping below comfortable before the next invoice clears
3. A flood of AI tools launching every week she can't keep up with
4. Job ads asking for "AI skills" she's not sure she has
5. Competitors charging triple what she does for similar work

### 🗣️ Saying
6. "I'm so busy but I'm not making enough money."
7. "I hate the sales part — I just want to do the work."
8. "I probably should be using AI more but I don't know where to start."
9. "I under-quoted again. I always do this."
10. "Maybe I should just go back and get a real job."

### 👂 Hearing
11. Her partner asking (gently) when the business will "settle down"
12. Business coaches online promising six figures with vague fluff
13. News headlines that "AI will replace creatives"
14. Friends saying "you're so lucky you work for yourself" (if only they knew)
15. Clients saying "can you do it cheaper?" and "my nephew uses Canva too"

### 🧠 Thinking
16. "Am I actually cut out for this, or was redundancy a sign?"
17. "If I don't figure out AI now, I'll be left behind."
18. "I don't have hours to waste on another course I won't finish."
19. "I need clients *this month*, not a five-year plan."
20. "I want to feel in control of my income for once."

---

## How the product answers Chloe

- **Inconsistent income →** predictable client pipeline system (Module 3)
- **Hates selling →** AI-scripted, low-pressure outreach & proposals (Modules 3–4)
- **Undercharging →** the Rate & Proposal Calculator (bonus tracker)
- **AI overwhelm →** one simple stack, not 40 tools (Module 1)
- **No time →** weekend-launch path + copy-paste templates
- **Fear of being replaced →** repositioned as "the freelancer who *commands* AI"
