# Prompt 8 — "Gemini-Ready" Mockup Prompt

Copy-paste the block below into Gemini (or any image/design AI). It's tuned to produce a 16:9 (1280×720) promotional mockup with catchy colours, fonts, attention-grabbing imagery, and a "Buy Now" CTA.

> **Note:** A fully working, editable version of this mockup already ships as `07-mockup/mockup.html` — open it in a browser to view/screenshot at exactly 1280×720. Use the Gemini prompt below to generate image variations.

---

## ✅ Copy-paste prompt

```
Create a high-converting promotional mockup image for a digital product, dimensions 16:9 (1280x720 pixels).

PRODUCT: "The AI Freelance Freedom Accelerator" — a digital course bundle for Australian freelancers that teaches them to land high-paying clients using AI in 30 days. It includes a video course, templates, and trackers.

AUDIENCE: Australian freelancers and side-hustlers, aged 25-45, who want freedom and a predictable income.

STYLE & MOOD: Modern, energetic, premium, trustworthy. Catchy and attention-grabbing but not cheap.

COLOURS: A bold gradient of violet/purple (#6C4DF6) to teal (#00C2A8), with warm gold/orange accents (#FFB020) for the call-to-action. High contrast, vibrant.

FONTS: A catchy, bold, modern sans-serif for the headline (e.g., Poppins/Montserrat Extra-Bold); a clean readable sans-serif for supporting text.

LAYOUT:
- LEFT SIDE: A small pill badge "🇦🇺 For Australian Freelancers · AI Edition". Below it, a huge bold headline "The AI Freelance Freedom Accelerator" with the word "Freedom" highlighted in gold. Under that, a subheadline "Land high-paying clients using AI — in just 30 days." Three feature pills: "🎥 Video Course", "🧰 Templates", "📊 Trackers". A prominent white rounded CTA button that says "BUY NOW — $199" with small text under it "Land a client in 30 days or money back". Below, a 5-star rating line "Loved by 1,200+ Aussie freelancers".
- RIGHT SIDE: A realistic laptop mockup showing a colourful course dashboard with module names, and a smaller smartphone mockup (tilted) showing a "Prompt Vault" app screen. Add a subtle glow and floating UI cards.

IMAGERY: Include attention-grabbing visual elements — subtle AI/tech motifs (nodes, sparkles), a faint grid, and soft glowing orbs in the background. Optionally an Australian visual cue (map silhouette or flag hint) done tastefully.

EXTRAS: Add a small "🔥 Founding-member launch price" ribbon/banner in a corner. Ensure the CTA stands out most. Everything must be crisp, legible, and conversion-focused.

Output a single polished 1280x720 image.
```

---

## 🎨 Variations to try
- Swap the gradient to **sunset (coral → gold)** for a warmer, lifestyle feel.
- Ask for a **dark-mode** version (deep navy background, neon accents) for a "tech" audience.
- Request a **story/reel version** at 9:16 (1080×1920) for Instagram/TikTok ads.
