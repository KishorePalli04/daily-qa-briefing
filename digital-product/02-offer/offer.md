## 2. The High-Converting Offer (Alex Hormozi "Grand Slam Offer" framework)

Hormozi's **Value Equation:**

> **Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)**

We maximise the top, minimise the bottom.

### Dream Outcome
> "Wake up to a full inbox of clients who happily pay your rates — while AI does the heavy lifting — so you finally have a calm, predictable freelance income you run in school hours."

### Increase Perceived Likelihood (proof & de-risking)
- Copy-paste scripts and templates (nothing to "figure out")
- Australia-specific (GST, ABN, local platforms, AUD pricing)
- Real case studies + a step-by-step 30-day roadmap
- **Guarantee:** "Land a paying client in 30 days or get a full refund **plus** $50 for your time."

### Reduce Time Delay
- **Quick-win in the first hour:** send your first AI-crafted pitch on Day 1
- Weekend-launch fast path for the time-poor

### Reduce Effort & Sacrifice
- Done-for-you templates and prompts
- "One simple AI stack," not 40 tools
- 5–10 minute lessons you can watch on your phone
