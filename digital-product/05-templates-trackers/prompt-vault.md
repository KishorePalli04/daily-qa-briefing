# 🧰 The Client-Getting Prompt Vault (Starter Set)

Copy-paste prompts for every stage of your freelance business. Replace anything in `[brackets]`.
This starter set is organised into 8 categories — add your own winning prompts underneath each.

> **Tip:** The best prompts follow **Role + Context + Format**. Give the AI a role, the specifics, and the exact output you want.

---

## 1. Positioning & Offer

**Positioning Generator**
> You are a brand strategist. My skill is [skill]. My ideal clients are [audience]. Give me 10 positioning statements in the format "I help [audience] get [outcome] without [pain]." Rank by market demand and pricing power.

**Niche Finder**
> List 20 profitable sub-niches for a freelance [skill] in Australia. For each, note typical budget, demand level, and how competitive it is. Recommend the top 3 for someone who wants premium clients.

**Signature Offer Builder**
> Help me design a signature offer for [audience]. Give it a name, a one-line promise, 3–5 deliverables, the transformation it creates, and a suggested AUD price range.

**Bio Rewrite**
> Rewrite my Instagram/LinkedIn bio. Skill: [skill]. Audience: [audience]. Make it clear, benefit-led, Australian tone, under 150 characters, with a soft CTA.

---

## 2. Finding Leads

**Lead Finder**
> Act as a lead-research assistant. My ideal client is [profile]. List 15 types of Australian businesses that fit, where to find them online, and 5 signals they can afford a freelancer.

**Ideal Client Profile**
> Build an ideal client profile for my [skill] service: industry, size, location, budget, buying triggers, and where they spend time online.

**Lead Qualifier Checklist**
> Create a 7-point checklist to decide if a prospect is worth pitching (signals of budget, need, and readiness to buy).

---

## 3. Outreach & Follow-up

**Warm Outreach DM**
> You are a friendly, non-pushy freelancer. Prospect details: [paste]. Write a 3-part outreach DM — personalised hook, genuine value observation, soft no-pressure ask. Under 80 words, Australian tone.

**Cold Email**
> Write a short cold email to [prospect] offering [service]. Lead with a specific observation about their business, one clear benefit, and a low-friction CTA (reply "yes" for a 2-min video). Subject line included.

**Follow-up Sequence**
> Write a 3-message follow-up sequence for a prospect who went quiet. Friendly, spaced over 10 days, each adding a small piece of value. No guilt-tripping.

**LinkedIn Connection Note**
> Write a 300-character LinkedIn connection request to [prospect] that references something specific about them and hints at how I help, without pitching.

---

## 4. Calls, Pricing & Proposals

**Discovery Call Script**
> Give me a discovery-call framework for [service]: opening, 8 questions to uncover their real problem and budget, and how to transition to presenting my offer.

**3-Tier Proposal**
> Write a proposal for [client] for [project]. Three tiers — Essential, Professional (recommended), Premium — each with deliverables, outcome, and AUD price. Add a short confidence guarantee.

**Objection Handler**
> Give me calm, confident responses to these objections: "It's too expensive," "Can you do it cheaper?," "I need to think about it," "My nephew uses Canva too."

**Rate Justifier**
> Help me explain my price to a client by framing it around the outcome and ROI, not hours. Project: [project]. Value to them: [value].

---

## 5. Content & Marketing

**Content Engine**
> Take this one client problem: [problem]. Turn it into a week of content: 1 LinkedIn post, 3 Instagram carousel outlines, 1 short video script, 1 email. Educational, Australian tone, each with a soft CTA.

**Hook Writer**
> Give me 10 scroll-stopping hooks for a post about [topic], aimed at [audience]. Mix curiosity, contrarian, and result-based angles.

**Case Study Post**
> Turn this project into a short "before/after" case study post: Client: [x]. Problem: [y]. What I did: [z]. Result: [result]. Keep it humble and specific.

**Repurpose**
> Turn this [post/call transcript/article] into 5 different formats for LinkedIn, Instagram, and an email. Keep my voice consistent.

---

## 6. Delivery & Client Experience

**Onboarding Email**
> Write a warm client onboarding email for [service]: welcome, what happens next, timeline, what I need from them, and how to reach me.

**Client Update**
> Turn these messy notes into a professional, reassuring progress update for my client: [notes].

**Call Summariser**
> Summarise this client call into: key decisions, action items (owner + due date), and open questions. Transcript: [paste].

**Handover Note**
> Write a friendly project handover message that lists deliverables, how to use them, and next-step suggestions.

---

## 7. Referrals & Retention

**Testimonial Request**
> Write a short, easy testimonial request for a happy client, including 3 guiding questions so they know what to say.

**Referral Ask**
> Write a natural referral request I can send at project end, plus a short blurb the client can forward to someone who'd benefit.

**Re-engagement**
> Write a friendly check-in to a past client I haven't spoken to in 3 months, offering a small helpful idea and re-opening the door to work together.

---

## 8. Business & Admin (Australia)

**Invoice Wording**
> Draft clear invoice wording for an Australian sole trader: my ABN [x], service [y], amount, GST shown separately if registered, 7-day terms, and payment details placeholder.

**Scope Guard**
> Write a polite message to handle scope creep: the client asked for [extra work] beyond our agreement. Reaffirm the value, and offer it as a paid add-on.

**Script Writer (for the course)**
> You are a calm, casual course presenter. Write a word-for-word 6–8 minute video script for the lesson "[lesson title]" aimed at Australian freelancers. End with one practical action step.
