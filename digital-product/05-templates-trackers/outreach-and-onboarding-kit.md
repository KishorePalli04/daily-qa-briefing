# ✉️ Outreach & Onboarding Kit (Templates)

Fill-in-the-blanks. Replace `[brackets]`. Keep it human — tweak so it sounds like *you*.

## Part 1 — Cold Outreach Message Kit

### Warm DM (Instagram / LinkedIn)
> Hey [Name] 👋 [Personal hook — something specific & genuine about them].
> I noticed [useful observation — a gap or opportunity]. That might be [quietly costing them X / an easy win].
> Happy to send a quick 2-min video showing what I'd tweak — no strings. Want me to?

### Cold Email
> **Subject:** Quick idea for [Business]
>
> Hi [Name],
> [Specific observation about their business]. I help [audience] [outcome], and I spotted one thing that could [benefit].
> Would it be silly to send a short 2-minute video walking through it? Just reply "yes" and it's on its way.
> Cheers,
> [Your name] · [ABN/website]

### Follow-up #1 (3 days later)
> Hey [Name], just floating this back to the top of your inbox 🙂 No pressure at all — happy to send that quick video whenever suits.

### Follow-up #2 (7 days later, add value)
> Hi [Name], thought of you — [share a quick, genuinely useful tip relevant to them]. If you ever want a hand putting it into action, I'm around.

## Part 2 — Client Onboarding Kit

### Welcome Email
> **Subject:** Welcome aboard — here's what happens next 🎉
>
> Hi [Name], so glad we're working together! Here's the plan so you know exactly what to expect:
> **1. Kickoff:** I'll send a short questionnaire (5 mins) so I fully understand your goals.
> **2. Timeline:** First draft by [date]; final by [date].
> **3. What I need from you:** [assets/logins/brand info].
> **4. Staying in touch:** I'll send updates every [X]. Reach me anytime at [email].
> Excited to get started!
> [Your name]

### Onboarding Questionnaire (paste into a form)
> 1. In one sentence, what does success look like for this project?
> 2. Who exactly is your ideal customer?
> 3. What do you love / want to avoid stylistically?
> 4. Any examples you admire (and why)?
> 5. What's the deadline and any key dates?
> 6. Anything else I should know?

### Progress Update
> Hi [Name], quick update 👇
> ✅ Done: [items]
> 🔄 In progress: [items]
> ⏭️ Next: [items]
> On track for [date]. Anything you'd like me to adjust? — [Your name]

## Part 3 — Offboarding Kit

### Handover Message
> Hi [Name], it's all ready! 🎉
> **Deliverables:** [links/files]
> **How to use them:** [1–2 lines or a short walkthrough video link]
> **Suggested next steps:** [ideas].
> It's been a pleasure — thank you for trusting me with this.

### Testimonial Request
> Hi [Name], so glad you're happy with the results! Would you be open to a short testimonial? To make it easy, feel free to answer any of these:
> • What was the problem before we worked together?
> • What was it like working with me?
> • What result did you get?
> Even 2–3 sentences would mean the world. 🙏

### Referral Ask
> Hi [Name], loved working on this with you. If you know anyone else who'd benefit from [service], I'd be grateful for an intro. Here's a line you can forward:
> *"I've been working with [Your name] on [service] — really impressed with the outcome. Might be worth a chat: [contact]."*
