# The AI Freelance Freedom Accelerator 🚀

A complete digital product, built end-to-end from the 8-prompt workflow in `AI_DIGITAL_PRODUCTS_.pdf`.

**Niche:** Australian freelancers, creators & side-hustlers who want to use AI to land high-paying clients and build a profitable one-person business.

**Product type:** A combination product — 🎥 video course + 🧰 templates + 📊 trackers.

---

| **1** — 10 product ideas | Trending product ideas + chosen product | `01-market-research/product-ideas.md` |

## 🧰 Templates & trackers (the deliverables customers download)
- `prompt-vault.md` — the Client-Getting Prompt Vault (starter set)
- `pipeline-tracker.csv` — track leads from first contact to won
- `income-gst-tracker.csv` — income, GST & tax set-aside (AU)
- `rate-calculator.csv` — work out your minimum rate & project floors
- `30-day-plan-tracker.csv` — one action a day for 30 days
- `outreach-and-onboarding-kit.md` — fill-in-the-blank client templates

## 👀 How to view the visual pieces
Open these in any browser:
- **Sales page:** `06-sales-page/sales-page.html`
- **Product mockup (1280×720):** `07-mockup/mockup.html`
- **eBook (printable):** `03-ebook/ebook.html`

`07-mockup/preview.png` is a rendered screenshot of the mockup.

